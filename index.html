The Profile

Who's behind the feed
A Delhi NCR–based beauty & lifestyle creator, telling honest beauty stories in Hinglish — with a genuine, first-love pull toward luxury beauty houses and the quiet discipline of a considered skincare ritual.

Shubhangi builds beauty and lifestyle content that speaks the way her audience actually talks — warm, candid, and unhurried. At the heart of her content instinct sits a real love for luxury beauty: the unboxing ritual, the ingredient story, the slow ten-step routine. Skincare, to her, is a daily ritual worth honouring properly, never a task to rush through.

That inclination toward premium beauty houses sits comfortably alongside an equally sincere drugstore instinct, so brands across the shelf — from mass favourites to maison names — find a genuine, well-cared-for home in her feed.

Three signature pillars anchor her storytelling and keep her audience returning, week over week.
